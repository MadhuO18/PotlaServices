Who we are

A group with a shared purpose

Headquartered in Hyderabad, Potla Group is a multi-business group serving organizations and communities through software development, consulting, staffing, digital media, and technology services.

We bring together skilled teams and practical experience to understand each client’s goals, deliver dependable work, and build partnerships that grow over time.

Our founder

Rajeshwari Potla

Rajeshwari Potla founded the group with a belief in quality, trust, and creating opportunities for people. Her vision continues to shape a culture grounded in values, service, and meaningful work.

That commitment to opportunity and community remains an important part of the group’s purpose as it grows across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and beyond.

Managing director

Srinu Potla

Srinu Potla leads the group’s work across technology, digital marketing, recruitment, corporate training, and telecom services. He brings together experienced teams to support clients with thoughtful planning and dependable delivery.

With a focus on collaboration and lasting partnerships, the group continues to build practical services around the needs of its clients and communities.
