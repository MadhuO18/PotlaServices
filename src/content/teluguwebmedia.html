About the publication

Regional perspectives, made accessible

Based in Hyderabad, Telugu Web Media is an independent digital news platform focused on the people, places, and events shaping Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. It brings regional perspectives into one accessible online destination for Telugu-speaking readers.

Its editorial team and contributors share timely regional reporting alongside stories on public affairs, entertainment, and culture. By covering subjects that matter to local communities, the publication helps readers stay informed about conversations and developments across the region.

Designed for a digital-first audience, Telugu Web Media makes it easy to discover stories wherever readers are—in their communities, elsewhere in India, or around the world. Its focus remains on relevant regional coverage and a strong connection to Telugu language and culture.
